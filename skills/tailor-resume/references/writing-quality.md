# Writing quality

Required guidance for drafting, tailoring, reviewing, and rewriting resume content. This file
packages the editing contracts from Improve Writing, Edit Russian text, Edit English text, and
Humanize content for use inside this plugin. It requires no other installed skills.

## Editing contract and priority

1. Preserve meaning, facts, requirements, authorship, ownership, uncertainty, and causality.
2. Preserve functional content: exact terminology, names, dates, figures, links, quotations,
   citations, code, formatting, metadata, and the requested document structure.
3. Correct language errors and internal inconsistencies.
4. Improve clarity, concision, information order, sentence construction, and flow.
5. Remove bureaucratic wording, assistant residue, empty rhetoric, and promotional inflation.
6. Preserve the author's voice and the formality appropriate to the document.

If a style correction reduces precision, restore the precise wording. Flag material ambiguities
instead of guessing. Do not research, verify, or expand factual claims as part of a language pass.
If factual verification is separately requested, keep it distinct from editing.

## Scope and modes

- Edit only the requested document, entry, bullets, or selected passage. Leave adjacent content
  untouched. Do not change headings, section order, or layout unless that is part of the request.
- In drafting or tailoring, use the facts and structural changes authorized by that workflow.
  In a narrow rewrite, keep entry metadata and all content outside the selection unchanged.
- In review mode, return findings with the exact quote or location, the problem, and a proposed
  correction. Do not silently rewrite the resume or hand a replacement to the Builder.
- When editing, return the revised text and only necessary unresolved questions or preservation
  conflicts. Follow the calling skill's requested fit report or change summary.
- If no meaningful improvement is needed, preserve the text and say so briefly.

## Required sequence

1. Identify the source language, requested output language, audience, tone, permitted scope,
   document type, protected elements, and employer form. Infer only what the input supports.
2. Establish the factual baseline. For a new resume, use the user's supplied facts; for an edit,
   use the original text. Keep names, figures, dates, attribution, uncertainty, and role ownership.
3. Edit each Russian or English segment using the relevant language pass below. Preserve proper
   names, product names, exact technical terms, quotations, and intentional code-switching.
   Translation is a separate, authorized step: preserve proficiency and strength of claims.
4. Run the final humanization pass below on the proposed text, retaining the source for comparison.
5. Run the preservation checklist and the canonical Markdown grammar check before delivery.

The plugin also supports Japanese documents. Apply the preservation and scope rules to them;
do not impose English sentence order, Russian punctuation, Western section order, or these
language-specific word lists on Japanese text. Retain the requested form and its field labels.

## Resume and surface calibration

- Use neutral professional language. Natural language does not require informality or emotion.
- Prefer specific actions and confirmed outcomes to vague duties. An action-result or XYZ bullet
  is useful only when its action, outcome, measurement, and causal link are supplied or confirmed.
  If only a duty is known, describe that duty precisely and ask about results outside the resume.
- Do not turn participation into leadership, team outcomes into individual outcomes, association
  into causation, or an estimate into an exact number. Preserve attribution and uncertainty.
- Use concise bullets, usually one or two lines, without forcing identical sentence lengths.
  Preserve fields, tables, and useful repetition required by an employer's form.
- Choose tense per action: past for completed work, including completed projects in a current
  role; present for ongoing responsibilities. Do not invent when an action ended.
- Keep one stable term for one concept. Retain truthful ATS keywords and exact qualifications;
  do not rotate synonyms or stuff the document with a posting's unsupported keywords.
- Keep questions, unconfirmed placeholders, editorial notes, and change reports outside the
  final resume. Do not delete valid canonical markers or actual structured fields as "residue".
- In commentary and reports, preserve required findings and useful explanations. In labels and
  metadata, preserve brevity and functional repetition. Do not make all surfaces sound alike.

## Russian language pass

- Correct spelling, punctuation, agreement, case government, word order, and unclear references.
- Replace abstract noun chains with direct verbs when meaning permits, especially chains of
  three or more dependent nouns. Split overloaded sentences and join artificial fragments.
- Use natural Russian constructions; remove English syntactic calques, rigid English word order,
  accidental language mixing, and untranslated model residue.
- Remove filler, repeated conclusions, unnecessary setup, serial linking words, and redundant
  self-references such as "как отмечалось выше" when they add no navigation value.
- Review bureaucratic or inflated wording: "является", "данный", "указанный", "вышеупомянутый",
  "осуществлять", "обеспечивать", "способствует", "позволяет", "демонстрирует", "свидетельствует",
  "в рамках", "представляет собой", "выступает в роли", "характеризуется", "играет ключевую роль",
  "важно отметить", "следует учитывать", "стоит подчеркнуть", "в современном мире", "не секрет,
  что", "всем известно", "таким образом", "подводя итог", and "в заключение".
- Review unsupported emphasis: "ключевой", "значительный", "существенный", "фундаментальный",
  "беспрецедентный", "уникальный", "инновационный", "революционный", "передовой", "бесшовный",
  and abstract "ландшафт", "экосистема", or "синергия". Replace with the concrete supplied fact.
- Remove hedging such as "возможно", "как правило", "в целом", or "в определённой степени" only
  when it is empty padding. Preserve any genuine uncertainty in the original claim.
- Remove staged sincerity ("скажем честно", "и знаете что?", "давайте будем честны", "давайте
  разберёмся") and rhetorical contrasts ("это не X, а Y", "не просто X, а Y", "не только X, но и Y")
  when they add rhetoric rather than necessary meaning.

## English language pass

- Correct spelling, punctuation, grammar, agreement, tense, referents, and sentence construction.
- Replace abstract nominalizations and noun chains with direct verbs when precision is preserved.
  Split overloaded sentences and join dramatic fragments; remove padding and repeated conclusions.
- Avoid decorative vocabulary unless it is exact terminology or a protected quotation: delve,
  tapestry, vibrant, robust, pivotal, testament, underscore, crucial, multifaceted, intricate,
  interplay, foster, enhance, bolster, garner, showcase, valuable, and abstract "landscape".
- Do not begin sentences with "Additionally" or "Notably". Prefer "is" and "are" to "serves as",
  "stands as", "features", "boasts", or "offers" when a simple copula is accurate.
- Remove assistant phrases such as "Certainly", "Great question", "Let's explore", and "I hope
  this helps"; staged sincerity such as "to be honest", "and you know what?", or "let's be honest";
  and formulaic contrasts such as "not just X, but Y" or "not only X, but also Y".
- Do not make a claim more certain, universal, important, or emotional than its source supports.

For both languages, these are editing checks, not indiscriminate word bans. Retain exact technical,
legal, organizational, product, and qualification terms and protected quotations. Use straight
quotes and apostrophes, sentence-case headings, and no decorative bold emphasis in prose. Replace
em dashes with commas, parentheses, colons, or separate sentences; preserve canonical syntax,
functional emphasis, proper names, and employer-supplied headings.

## Final humanization pass

Remove or flag a clear pattern even if it appears only once:

- Model identity, leaked instructions, tool output, citation syntax leakage, internal process
  notes, assistant disclaimers, generic greetings, offers to help, and closing filler.
- Unfilled placeholders, accidental editorial notes, hidden production instructions, and
  template residue. Preserve valid frontmatter, entry labels, block attributes, and containers.
- Unsupported authority or vague attribution ("experts believe", "research shows", "industry
  reports say", "эксперты считают", "исследования показывают", "по данным аналитиков", "практика
  показывает") without a named source; false agency ("the data speaks", "the market dictates",
  "данные говорят", "рынок диктует", "система решает") when the real actor can be named.
- Invented specificity, evidence, metrics, examples, names, dates, product states, or personal
  experience; any change of author, speaker, organization, or owner of an action or outcome.
- Staged sincerity, manufactured intimacy, fake uncertainty, and imitated spontaneity.
- Forced triplets, rhetorical contrasts, decorative synonym rotation, fake ranges with unrelated
  endpoints, repeated sentence templates, uniform paragraph openings, and mechanical rhythm.
- Promotional praise, unsupported superlatives, motivational language, inflated significance,
  travel-guide language, restated points, and empty openings or conclusions.
- Unrequested hooks, calls to like, save, share, comment, or keep reading, and formulaic final
  sections such as "Challenges", "Future outlook", or "Key takeaways".

Never add anecdotes, memories, emotions, reactions, metaphors, idioms, jokes, slang, deliberate
mistakes, artificial fragments, sensory details, first-person opinions, or uncertainty merely to
sound human. Do not guess which model wrote the text or provide an AI-authorship score.

## Final preservation checklist

Compare the result with the factual baseline and confirm:

- The central claim, purpose, logical relationships, and causal strength are unchanged.
- Facts, numbers, dates, quotations, attribution, requirements, conditions, exceptions, warnings,
  qualifications, and genuine uncertainty remain intact.
- The candidate's role and ownership of every action, opinion, experience, and result are unchanged.
- No unsupported detail, metric, personal stake, emotion, example, or conclusion has been added.
- Terminology is stable; exact keywords have not been replaced with decorative synonyms.
- Language, authorial voice, professional formality, and the employer's form remain appropriate.
- Links, citations, code, headings, metadata, canonical markers, containers, and untouched content
  are preserved within the authorized scope. The final Markdown still parses under the grammar.
- No clear assistant residue, promotional inflation, mechanical filler, or simulated humanity remains.

If a cleanup changes precise meaning or required syntax, keep the precise wording and flag the
conflict. Raise material missing facts outside the final resume; never fill them by invention.

## Worked examples

These examples contain only the stated facts; the edit adds no result or metric.

| Supplied text                                              | Revised text                                               | Boundary                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Responsible for preparing weekly sales reports."          | "Prepared weekly sales reports."                           | No invented business impact.                             |
| "Supported the team that reduced review time by 15%."      | "Supported a team effort that reduced review time by 15%." | The outcome remains the team's.                          |
| "Осуществлял подготовку еженедельных отчетов по продажам." | "Готовил еженедельные отчеты по продажам."                 | Same duty, direct verb.                                  |
| "Помог команде сократить время проверки примерно на 15%."  | "Помог команде сократить время проверки примерно на 15%."  | Preserve ownership and approximation; no edit is needed. |
